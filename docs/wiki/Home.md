UAMSWP Find-a-Doc is the WordPress plugin behind the Find-a-Doc directory: the provider, location, area of expertise, condition, treatment and clinical resource content types, the templates that display them, the editing roles that maintain them, and the sync that keeps Google Business Profile listings in step with them.

This wiki holds the long-form documentation: a walkthrough, task guides, reference pages and troubleshooting. The [README](https://github.com/UAMS-Web/UAMSWP-Find-a-Doc#readme) keeps the overview, the requirements, installation and the development notes.
